Snap for Jones IT Privacy Policy

Updated October 15, 2026.

Snap for Jones IT is an internal Jones IT edition of the Snap for Harvest Chrome extension. It lets Jones IT employees log time to Jones IT Tracker or to Harvest from the page they are working on. Each person chooses which one Snap uses.

What Snap reads

When you open Snap on a page, it reads that page's address (URL) and title, or its visible heading, to fill in the notes and documentation link for a time entry. You can edit both before saving. Snap does not read this until you open it. It does not read page contents beyond the title or heading, and it does not read cookies, passwords, or form fields. It does not collect your browsing history or send information about pages where you don't open Snap.

Jones IT Tracker mode

In Tracker mode Snap talks only to Jones IT's Tracker service at tracker.itjones.com, over HTTPS. It sends:

  • The page address and your notes while you prepare an entry, so Tracker can suggest a client, project, and task.
  • The time entries you save, start, switch, or stop: date, duration, project, task, notes, documentation link, billable status, and whether to copy the entry to Harvest.

To connect, you sign in with your Jones IT Google account on the Tracker website and approve the request. Snap then receives a personal, revocable connection key for your own time and assigned projects. The key is kept in the extension's local storage on your device, where web pages cannot read it. To open faster, Snap keeps a copy of your project list and running timer in Chrome's session storage, which is cleared when you close the browser.

If you choose Copy to Harvest, Tracker sends that entry to Jones IT's Harvest account.

Harvest mode

In Harvest mode Snap opens Harvest's own timer from platform.harvestapp.com and passes it the page address and title. You sign in to Harvest directly, and Snap never sees your Harvest password or account data. Snap watches for Harvest's own "timer stopped" requests only to update its button on your device. Harvest's handling of your data is covered by Harvest's privacy policy.

Stored on your device

  • Your settings, such as which platform to use, enabled sites, and the Snap button position, are stored in Chrome's sync storage. Chrome may sync them across your own devices.
  • Your Tracker connection key is stored in the extension's local storage.
  • Site icons next to documentation links come from Chrome's own icon cache on your device.

What Snap does not do

Snap has no analytics, advertising, or tracking, and it does not sell or share your data. It does not run code downloaded from the internet. Its settings page loads fonts from Google Fonts.

Who can see your time

Jones IT administrators can review Tracker time records and their change history. Time copied to or logged in Harvest is visible to Jones IT's Harvest account administrators.

Disconnecting and retention

You can disconnect Tracker from Snap's settings or revoke the connection on Tracker's Connections page. Disconnecting removes the key from your device but does not delete time you already saved. Tracker keeps time records, their history, and backups as Jones IT's work record.
