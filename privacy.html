Tracker by Jones IT Privacy Policy

Updated October 20, 2026.

Tracker by Jones IT is Jones IT's internal Chrome extension for logging time to Jones IT Tracker from the page an employee is working on. It was previously named Snap for Jones IT.

What the extension reads

When you open the Tracker panel on a page, the extension reads that page's address (URL) and title, or its visible heading, to fill in the notes and documentation link for a time entry. You can edit both before saving. It does not read this until you open the panel. It does not read page contents beyond the title or heading, and it does not read cookies, passwords, or form fields. It does not collect your browsing history or send information about pages where you don't open the panel.

What it sends to Tracker

The extension talks only to Jones IT's Tracker service at tracker.itjones.com, over HTTPS. It sends:

  • The page address and your notes while you prepare an entry, so Tracker can suggest a client, project, and task.
  • The time entries you save, start, switch, or stop: date, duration, project, task, notes, documentation link, billable status, and whether to copy the entry to Harvest.

To connect, you sign in with your Jones IT Google account on the Tracker website and approve the request. The extension then receives a personal, revocable connection key for your own time and assigned projects. The key is kept in the extension's local storage on your device, where web pages cannot read it. To open faster, the extension keeps a copy of your project list and running timer in Chrome's session storage, which is cleared when you close the browser.

If you choose Copy to Harvest, Tracker sends that entry to Jones IT's Harvest account. The extension itself does not connect to Harvest.

Stored on your device

  • Your settings, such as enabled sites, integrations, and the Tracker button position, are stored in Chrome's sync storage. Chrome may sync them across your own devices.
  • Your Tracker connection key is stored in the extension's local storage.
  • Site icons next to documentation links come from Chrome's own icon cache on your device.

What the extension does not do

It has no analytics, advertising, or tracking, and it does not sell or share your data. It does not run code downloaded from the internet, and its fonts and icons are bundled with it.

Who can see your time

Jones IT administrators can review Tracker time records and their change history. Time copied to Harvest is visible to Jones IT's Harvest account administrators.

Disconnecting and retention

You can disconnect from the extension's settings (Account tab) or revoke the connection on Tracker's Connections page. Disconnecting removes the key from your device but does not delete time you already saved. Tracker keeps time records, their history, and backups as Jones IT's work record.
